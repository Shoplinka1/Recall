PRODUCT NAME
------------

Recall

TAGLINE
-------

"Stop rereading. Find what you don't know."

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
-------------------

Recall is an intelligent AI study and practice workspace.

The user gives Recall their own learning material — PDFs, lecture slides, documents, images, pasted notes, or text.

Recall understands the material, identifies topics and concepts, creates grounded practice questions, evaluates the student's answers, identifies weaknesses, and continuously generates personalized practice based on what the student actually does and does not understand.

The product MUST NOT feel like a generic ChatGPT wrapper.

The core experience is:

MATERIAL
   ↓
UNDERSTAND
   ↓
PRACTICE
   ↓
DIAGNOSE
   ↓
IDENTIFY WEAKNESSES
   ↓
TARGETED PRACTICE
   ↓
MEASURE IMPROVEMENT
   ↓
RECOMMEND WHAT TO DO NEXT

The goal is to make the student have the feeling:

"Holy shit, this actually knows what I don't know."

IMPORTANT PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
----------------------------

Existing products such as Quizlet, Anki, Jungle, NotebookLM, ChatGPT, etc. are NOT reasons to avoid building Recall.

Competition validates that students want this category.

Do NOT attempt to copy these products feature-for-feature.

Recall should win through:

1. Better learning loop.
2. Better diagnosis.
3. Better personalization.
4. Better weakness targeting.
5. Better progress visibility.
6. Better simplicity.
7. Better user experience.
8. Better explanation of WHY the student is weak.
9. Better recommendations for what to study next.
10. A product that feels like an intelligent personal study coach rather than a question generator.

DO NOT turn Recall into a bloated all-in-one education platform.

CORE PRODUCT LOOP
-----------------

The most important loop in the entire application is:

Upload material
→ understand material
→ generate grounded practice
→ answer questions
→ diagnose knowledge
→ identify weak concepts
→ generate targeted practice
→ compare performance
→ update mastery
→ recommend next action.

Everything else is secondary.

AI GROUNDING SYSTEM
-------------------

This is one of the most important technical requirements.

Recall MUST NOT randomly generate questions that merely sound related to the student's subject.

Questions must be grounded in the student's uploaded material.

Every generated question must internally maintain:

- source material
- source section
- source page where available
- source excerpt
- concept
- difficulty
- question type

For example:

Question:
"What is the function of the sinoatrial node?"

Source:
Anatomy Lecture 4
Page 12
Excerpt: "...the sinoatrial node..."

Concept:
Cardiac conduction

Difficulty:
Medium

This information should be stored in the database.

When possible, the student should be able to click:

"View source"

and see the relevant source material/excerpt.

If the AI cannot find sufficient evidence in the user's material, it should NOT invent the answer.

Instead:

"Not enough information in your material to create a reliable question."

QUALITY OVER QUESTION COUNT.

DIAGNOSIS LOGIC
---------------

After a practice session:

1. Calculate raw performance.
2. Group answers by concept.
3. Account for difficulty.
4. Account for confidence.
5. Identify repeated errors.
6. Identify misconception patterns.
7. Update mastery.
8. Recommend next action.

Example:

Concept: Active Transport
Correct: 2/6
Confidence: high on incorrect answers

Diagnosis:

"Possible misconception detected."

This should be more useful than:

"You got 4 wrong."

AI should produce concise, actionable diagnoses.
