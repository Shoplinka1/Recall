CORE PRODUCT GOAL:

Recall is not just a PDF-to-quiz generator.

A user uploads their own study material, and Recall should act like an intelligent study tutor: understand the material, identify important concepts, test understanding in different ways, recognize what the learner knows and does not know, and make practice progressively more useful.

QUESTION GENERATION:

1. Generate as many genuinely useful questions as the source supports, rather than an arbitrary fixed number.
2. For substantial material, identify and cover multiple important concepts and sections.
3. Prioritize important learning concepts instead of randomly sampling sentences.
4. Prevent exact duplicate questions.
5. Detect and reject near-duplicate questions testing essentially the same knowledge.
6. Avoid repeatedly asking for the same answer or concept.
7. Vary question forms when supported:
   - recall/definition
   - explanation
   - mechanism
   - cause/effect
   - comparison
   - application
   - scenario
   - relationship
   - MCQ
   - true/false
   - short-answer
8. Prefer questions that test understanding, reasoning and application rather than repeated memorization.
9. Never invent information outside the user's material.
10. Every question and expected answer must be grounded in the persisted source.
11. Preserve valid section/source provenance.
12. MCQ options must be plausible, unambiguous and source-grounded.
13. True/false statements must be genuinely supported by the source.
14. Short answers must remain compatible with the Step 5 answer evaluator.
15. Preserve PostgreSQL/Drizzle persistence, authentication and ownership.

LEARNING BEHAVIOR:

Use the existing Step 6 mastery/weakness information when it is already available.

Recall should avoid wasting practice on concepts the learner repeatedly demonstrates strong mastery of.

When the learner struggles:
- give useful additional practice on that concept
- vary the way the concept is tested
- prefer understanding/application questions where the source supports them
- do not simply repeat the exact failed question

When the learner demonstrates mastery:
- reduce unnecessary repetition
- move toward broader connections and application when supported by the material
